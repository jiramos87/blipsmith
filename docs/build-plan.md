- 2026-07-24: B08 implemented, the instrument can now describe itself. `features.cpp` measures all six pinned keys on the post-bus samples: peak/RMS dBFS through a new deterministic `linear_to_db`, pitch by McLeod-normalized autocorrelation with parabolic interpolation (within 0.1% almost everywhere, 1.75% worst case on hard-edged waveforms at 3.5 kHz), spectral centroid over Hann-windowed 1024-point FFTs, attack time as time-to-peak. **kissfft was not vendored**: its twiddles come from libm `cos`/`sin`, which the no-libm gate rejects, so the FFT is 40 lines of radix-2 reading exact twiddles from the B04 sine table (Note recorded on B08). Two wrong turns worth remembering: dividing autocorrelation by the overlap count to remove the taper bias caused catastrophic sub-octave errors (the taper is what suppresses spurious long lags), and the McLeod threshold had to drop to 0.75 for aliasing waveforms at high pitch. `explain.py` renders a deterministic plain-language description naming waveform, pitch, envelope shape, and only the stages actually switched on; `render()` now populates `RenderResult.explanation`. Replaced B03's null-feature assertion. 744 tests green. Next: `/implement docs/backlogs/blipsmith-v1.md B09`.
